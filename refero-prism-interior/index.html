01 / CONTENT AS LANDMARK

Read the wall
you are facing.

Instead of opening a detached information panel, each surface owns its own writing. Direction and content become the same cue, which should reduce the “where am I?” problem of an interior 3D view.

Settle first, write secondText starts writing only after the view has reached a stable wall. Camera motion and long-form reading do not compete.
